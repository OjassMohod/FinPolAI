# POLICY-REVIEW-06: Higher-value review

Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01

FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION POLICY — not actual banking regulations.

Transfers strictly above INR 500,000; USD 8,000; EUR 6,000; SGD 10,000; GBP 5,000 require manual review when no higher priority violation or missing information applies. This is an advisory assessment; no payment is executed.
