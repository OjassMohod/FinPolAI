# POLICY-MANUAL-08: Investment and uncertain assessments

Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01

FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION POLICY — not actual banking regulations.

An investment payment requires manual compliance review. Overall precedence is FLAGGED, then NEEDS INFORMATION, then REVIEW REQUIRED, then PASS. PASS is possible only when all applicable mandatory requirements are resolved. Document retrieval and LLM text never override structured rules.
