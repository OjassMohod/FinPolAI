# POLICY-DOCS-04: Supporting documentation

Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01

FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION POLICY — not actual banking regulations.

Transfers strictly above INR 200,000; USD 3,000; EUR 2,500; SGD 4,000; GBP 2,000 require source_of_funds. An explicitly empty document list establishes that a required document is absent. An unknown document list needs information. Business transactions also require an invoice regardless of value.
