# POLICY-TRANSFER-01: International transfer limits

Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01

FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION POLICY — not actual banking regulations.

All amounts are evaluated in their original currency. Fictional per-transfer caps are INR 1,000,000; USD 15,000; EUR 12,000; SGD 20,000; GBP 10,000. Amounts equal to a cap pass. No currency conversion is performed. Only these five currencies are supported.
