# POLICY-VERIFY-02: Identity and transaction completeness

Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01

FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION POLICY — not actual banking regulations.

Sender and recipient names, sender and recipient countries, amount, currency, purpose, customer type, verification statuses and the available-document list must be known. Sender KYC and recipient verification must both be complete. An unknown verification status needs information; a known incomplete status is a violation.
