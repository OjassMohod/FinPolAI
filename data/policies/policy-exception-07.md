# POLICY-EXCEPTION-07: Scholarship documentation exception

Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01

FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION POLICY — not actual banking regulations.

For purpose tuition, if both scholarship_letter and enrollment_letter are supplied, the source_of_funds requirement is waived. This exception does not waive transfer caps, identity verification, corridor checks, invoices or high-value review. Unknown exception facts must not be presumed true.
